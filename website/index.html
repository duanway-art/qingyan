What it does

From one stick of incense to one lineage

Offering

Kowtow, incense, and a plate

A kowtow adds one zhang. Plain incense and the plain offering are free. The longer an incense burns, the less smoke it adds each hour. You cannot light another until the countdown ends.

Lineage

Parents, children, spouses

Write a name and the years of birth and death. Leave the year of death empty, and the person is living. Elders sit above, descendants below, spouses side by side. The whole tree can be shared as an image.

Chronicle

Each day, kept

The number in a cell is that day’s real total. Skyward starts at 300 zhang, and anything above that is still recorded.

The grave

The earth mound is free

A day counts only when its smoke reaches 300 zhang. Ten such days unlock the stone grave, twenty the brick grave, and thirty the stele pavilion. Heading and inscription are four characters each.

Incense

Patron only unlocks finer incense

Patron is a one-time purchase. It is not a subscription, has no trial, and does not renew. It unlocks five finer incenses and five finer offerings. Plain incense and the plain offering stay free.

Reminders

When the incense ends

The app can remind you when incense ends, when an offering ends, and once a day. If you have already visited that day, the daily reminder stays quiet.
